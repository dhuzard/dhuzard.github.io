Welfare & anomaly detection¶

Because the DVC records continuously and undisturbed, a drop in activity is often an early, sensitive signal of compromised welfare — frequently more sensitive than body weight, and detectable before a human observer would notice. These analyses turn that signal into an automatic welfare-triage readout.

An orientation tool, not a clinical diagnosis

Anomaly and welfare outputs are exploratory — they are designed to flag cages worth a closer look, not to diagnose. Treat a low health score as “go and check this cage”, never as a medical conclusion.

Rolling-baseline anomaly detection¶

What it tells you. The tool learns each cage’s own recent normal activity and flags time bins that deviate sharply from it — capturing both unusual quiet (possible illness, pain, post-procedure recovery) and unusual bursts.

How it works.

  1. Rolling baseline — a trailing (causal, past-only) rolling mean and standard deviation of binned activity over a window (default 7 days). Because it only looks backwards, it never “sees the future” of the series.

  2. Anomaly flag — each bin gets a z-score, (activity − baseline mean) / baseline sd, and is flagged when its magnitude exceeds a threshold (default 2). Bins before the baseline has warmed up are left unflagged rather than guessed.

Tools: rolling_baseline, detect_anomalies, plot_anomaly_timeline.

Daily welfare / health score¶

What it tells you. A single, easy-to-read number per cage per day, on a 0–100 scale, summarising how anomalous that day was. 100 = no anomalous bins; lower = more deviation from the cage’s own baseline. This is what a daily monitor can email you (see Daily monitoring & email alerts).

How it’s computed. The day’s anomaly rate is the fraction of bins flagged as anomalous; the health score is (1 − anomaly rate) × 100.

Tool: daily_health_score.

Why activity is a good welfare readout¶

The choice of activity as a welfare signal is well supported in the DVC literature:

  • Colitis — DVC activity tracked disease severity as well as gold-standard clinical scoring, and detected handling/restraint stress (Zentrich et al. 2021, 10.3389/fnins.2021.760606).

  • Post-surgery — after intracranial surgery, activity (especially dark-phase) was more sensitive than body weight for detecting compromised welfare, and flagged individual aberrant patterns (Abdollahi Nejat et al. 2024, 10.3389/fnbeh.2024.1457894).

  • Cancer models — locomotion declined with tumour progression, suggesting the DVC can flag disease progression and test therapies, even for group-housed cages (Tomanelli et al. 2024, 10.3389/fonc.2024.1504938).

Together these support integrating continuous home-cage monitoring into routine severity assessment — a concrete contribution to the 3Rs (refinement).

Data sanity — the quality gate¶

Separately from welfare, the tool runs a data-sanity traffic-light check (green / amber / red) for missing data, irregular sampling and coverage problems. It’s exposed in the app (step 7), on the command line (udwa sanity), and as the engine behind daily monitoring. A red verdict blocks export unless you explicitly override it — so a quality problem can’t quietly slip into a published result.

See The command line and Daily monitoring & email alerts.
