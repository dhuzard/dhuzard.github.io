Literature references¶

Every analysis in this tool is grounded in the peer-reviewed Digital Ventilated Cage literature. This page collects the full citations so you can read and cite the primary sources in your own methods and discussion.

Source of truth

This list mirrors the curated, offline corpus in udwa/dvc_literature.py (15 studies, 5 Tecniplast-affiliated), also rendered as docs/DVC_LITERATURE_KB.md. The in-app Related literature panel ranks this corpus against your analysis and runs fully offline. Always read and cite the primary article (by DOI) before relying on a finding.

Method foundations¶

These are the studies that establish the specific methods this tool implements.

Method

Reference

Circadian battery (cosinor, IS/IV/RA, bouts, onsets, periodogram, sleep)

Tir S, Foster RG, Peirson SN (2025). Evaluation of the Digital Ventilated Cage system for circadian phenotyping. Sci Rep. 10.1038/s41598-025-87530-6

Regularity Disruption Index (RDI) & excessive active-phase rest

Golini E, Rigamonti M, Raspa M, Scavizzi F, et al. (2023). Excessive rest time during active phase … myotonic dystrophy type 1 … home cage monitoring. Front Behav Neurosci. 10.3389/fnbeh.2023.1130055

Spatial / trajectory metrics

Shenk J, Lohkamp KJ, Wiesmann M, Kiliaan AJ (2020). Automated Analysis of Stroke Mouse Trajectory Data With Traja. Front Neurosci. 10.3389/fnins.2020.00518

Welfare / severity from activity

Zentrich E, Talbot SR, Bleich A, Häger C (2021). Automated Home-Cage Monitoring During Acute Experimental Colitis in Mice. Front Neurosci. 10.3389/fnins.2021.760606

Activity vs body weight for welfare (post-surgery)

Abdollahi Nejat M, Stiedl O, Smit AB, van Kesteren RE (2024). Continuous locomotor activity monitoring to assess animal welfare following intracranial surgery in mice. Front Behav Neurosci. 10.3389/fnbeh.2024.1457894

Group-housed circadian profiles

Sun R, Gaerz MC, Oeing C, Mai K, Brachs S (2024). Accurate locomotor activity profiles of group-housed mice derived from home cage monitoring data. Front Neurosci. 10.3389/fnins.2024.1456307

Urine excretion (UrinatoR)

Dall M, et al. (2025). UrinatoR … urine excretion from home-cage humidity. Lab Anim. 10.1038/s41684-025-01648-8 · source

Named statistical methods used but not DVC-specific (and therefore uncited in the code): the cosinor model (Halberg), the van Someren non-parametric actigraphy descriptors, the Sokolove–Bushell chi-square periodogram, the Lomb–Scargle periodogram, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control, and the Mann–Whitney / Kruskal–Wallis / Shapiro–Wilk tests. Consult a standard chronobiology or statistics reference for these.
