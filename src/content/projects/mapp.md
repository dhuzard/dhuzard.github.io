MAPP is an API-first platform that makes preclinical metadata findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable — and aligned with the 3Rs. The platform is being built around a FAIRRR philosophy: **FAIR for the Animals, and for Innovative, Reproducible, and Responsible Research**.
