Developed by Damien Huzard in 2026, the **Ethical Debt of Preclinical Research** framework describes the avoidable ethical liability created when animal-derived evidence loses reusable scientific value through inadequate metadata, provenance, preservation, accessibility, or reproducibility. It is derived from the **WellFAIR/data welfare** concept co-developed with Benoit Petit-Demoulière.

Canonical framework: [neuronautix.com/ethical-debt](https://neuronautix.com/ethical-debt/)

Early public dissemination: [*The Ethical Debt of Preclinical Research: When Poor Data Stewardship Wastes Animal Lives*](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ethical-debt-preclinical-research-when-poor-data-wastes-damien-huzard-bmwae/), 25 June 2026.
