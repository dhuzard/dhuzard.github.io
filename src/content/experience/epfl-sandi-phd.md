- Studied a rat model selected for differential adaptation profiles to stress (Low / Inter / High).
- Validated the lines as a way to study neurodevelopment disorders related to stress pathways.
- Demonstrated that both Low and High lines had abnormal heart-rate variability (cardiac susceptibility).
- High-line rats had better working memory after aging ("stress helps consolidate memories"), while Low-line rats showed impaired learning and reversal deficits.
- Explored social buffering and social contagion across rats with different "personalities".
